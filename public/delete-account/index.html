Delete your FaivoPay account

FaivoPay is operated by NXT Marketing Solutions Limited.

You can request deletion of your FaivoPay driver account and associated personal information.

Request deletion through the app

Sign in to FaivoPay and open Account → About FaivoPay → Request account deletion.

Confirm the request. It will be submitted for administrator review.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email our privacy team from your registered email address. Include a request to delete your FaivoPay account.

Request account deletion

Email: privacy@faivopay.app

What happens to your information?

We will review the request, verify your identity where necessary, and delete account information that is no longer required.

This may include your account credentials, registration information and associated personal profile information.

Payment, payout, settlement, transaction and accounting records may be retained where required for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention or legitimate record-keeping obligations.

What happens next?

Our team will review your request and contact you if further information is required. We will explain the outcome and any information that must be retained.

Important: Submitting a request does not immediately close your account, cancel pending payments or remove financial obligations.
